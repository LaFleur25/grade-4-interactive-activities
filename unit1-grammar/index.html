Daily routines + Present Simple + expressions and adverbs of frequency

📘 Learn the Grammar

Present Simple tells us about routines and things we do regularly.

I / You / We / They + base verb → I make my bed every day.
He / She / It + verb + -s → Kim gets up at eight o'clock.
How often...? asks about frequency.
We can answer with always, usually, sometimes, never or expressions such as every day, once a week, twice a week, three times a week.
🎒 I pack my bag every day.
🐶 She feeds her pet every morning.
🧹 We sometimes pick up our rooms.
⏰ He is never late for school.
Remember! Frequency adverbs usually come before an action verb: I usually do my homework.
With be, they come after am/is/are: She is sometimes late.
