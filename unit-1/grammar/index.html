📘 Present Simple

Learn how we talk about routines, habits and things that happen regularly.

1️⃣ When do we use the Present Simple?

We use the Present Simple to talk about routines, habits and things that happen regularly.
🌞 I make my bed every morning.
🎒 We go to school every day.
🐶 Ben feeds his pet after school.

2️⃣ How do we make positive sentences?

I / You / We / They
+ VERB
📚 We read a book.
🦷 They brush their teeth.
He / She / It
+ VERB + S
⏰ He gets up early.
🎒 She packs her school bag.
👀 Watch out!
go → goes • do → does • brush → brushes • have → has

3️⃣ Watch the Verb! 🔎

With he, she and it, the verb usually changes a little.

Most verbs → + s
read → reads
get → gets
-sh / -ch / -o → + es
brush → brushes
go → goes
consonant + y → y becomes ies
study → studies
try → tries
Special one
have → has
She has breakfast at seven.
💡 Remember: We use these changes with he / she / it and one person, such as Alex or Emma.

6️⃣ Adverbs of Frequency

🌟 always — 100%   →   I always do my homework.
😊 usually — 75%   →   I usually get up at seven.
🙂 sometimes — 50%   →   I sometimes read a book.
🌙 almost never — 25%   →   I almost never go to bed late.
🚫 never — 0%   →   I never forget my school bag.

We can also say:

📅 every day
1️⃣ once a week
2️⃣ twice a week
3️⃣ three times a week

1️⃣ How do we make negative sentences?

I / You / We / They
don't + VERB
🌙 I don't go to bed late.
📚 You don't read before school.
🎒 We don't go to school on Sunday.
🥣 They don't have breakfast at school.
He / She / It
doesn't + VERB
📚 He doesn't read before school.
⏰ She doesn't get up late.
🐶 It doesn't sleep outside.
🦷 Alex doesn't brush his teeth after lunch.
⭐ Important: After doesn't, use the verb without -s.
❌ He doesn't gets up late.
✅ He doesn't get up late.

2️⃣ From Positive to Negative

Look at how the sentence changes. Tap Show Answer to check.

⏰ Alex gets up at seven.
Alex doesn't get up at seven.
📚 Emma reads after school.
Emma doesn't read after school.
🎒 We go to school on Saturday.
We don't go to school on Saturday.
🥣 They have breakfast at school.
They don't have breakfast at school.
⭐ Notice: gets → doesn't get and reads → doesn't read. After doesn't, the verb goes back to its simple form.

1️⃣ How do we make questions?

I / you / we / they
Do + subject + VERB?
📚 Do you read a book?
🎒 Do we go to school at eight?
🥣 Do they have breakfast at home?
📝 Do you do your homework after school?
he / she / it
Does + subject + VERB?
📚 Does she read a book?
⏰ Does he get up at seven?
🐶 Does it sleep inside?
🎒 Does Alex go to school at eight?
⭐ Important: After does, use the verb without -s.
❌ Does Alex goes to school?
✅ Does Alex go to school?

2️⃣ How often...?

How often asks about frequency.

📚 How often do you read a book? → Twice a week.
📝 How often does Mia do her homework? → Every day.

3️⃣ Question Words

We can put a question word before do or does when we want more information.

⏰ What time
What time do you get up?
📅 When
When do you do your homework?
📍 Where
Where does Ben have breakfast?
🔁 How often
How often do you read a book?

4️⃣ Short Answers

For Do / Does questions, we can give short answers.

Do you read after school?
✅ Yes, I do.    ❌ No, I don't.

Does Emma get up at seven?
✅ Yes, she does.    ❌ No, she doesn't.
💡 We don't repeat the main verb in a short answer.
Does Alex read? → Yes, he does.

5️⃣ Mini Q&A

Read the information and choose the correct short answer.

📚 Emma reads a book every evening.
Does Emma read a book every evening?
🌙 Alex goes to bed at nine. He doesn't go to bed late.
Does Alex go to bed late?
