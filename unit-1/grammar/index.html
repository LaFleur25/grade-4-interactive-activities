📘 Present Simple & Frequency

1️⃣ When do we use the Present Simple?
We use the Present Simple to talk about routines, habits and things that happen regularly.

🌞 I make my bed every morning.
🎒 We go to school every day.
🐶 Ben feeds his pet after school.
2️⃣ I / You / We / They
Use the verb in its basic form.
I / You / We / They + verb
🎒 I pack my bag for school.
📚 They study for a test.
🏠 We go home after school.
🧹 You pick up your room.
3️⃣ He / She / It
We usually add -s to the verb.
He / She / It + verb + s
⏰ Kim gets up at eight.
🥪 She makes her lunch.
🐕 Alex feeds his pet.
🎒 He packs his bag.
⭐ Watch out!
Some verbs change a little:
go → goes   •   do → does
Example: Coco goes to school at eight o'clock.
4️⃣ How often...?
We use How often...? to ask how frequently someone does something.

❓ How often do you do your homework?
💬 I do my homework every day.

With he / she / it, use does:
❓ How often does Alex study for a test?
5️⃣ Adverbs of Frequency

🌟 always — 100%   →   I always do my homework.
😊 usually — 75%   →   I usually get up at seven.
🙂 sometimes — 50%   →   I sometimes read a book.
🌙 almost never — 25%   →   I almost never go to bed late.
🚫 never — 0%   →   I never forget my school bag.

We can also say:
📅 every day
1️⃣ once a week
2️⃣ twice a week
3️⃣ three times a week
💡 Where does the frequency word go?

Before most action verbs:
I usually do my homework after dinner.
She sometimes feeds her pet in the morning.

But after am / is / are:
She is never late for school.
🎯 Quick recap
✔ Use Present Simple for routines.
✔ Add -s with he, she and it.
✔ Use do with I/you/we/they and does with he/she/it in “How often...?” questions.
✔ Use words such as always, usually, sometimes, almost never, never to tell how often something happens.
