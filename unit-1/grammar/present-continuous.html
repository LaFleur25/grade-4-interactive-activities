📘 Present Continuous

We use the Present Continuous for actions happening NOW.

Emma is reading a book now.
They are having lunch at the moment.

⏰ Time Expressions

now right now at the moment
📖 Emma is reading a book now.
📝 Ben is doing his homework right now.
🍽️ They are having lunch at the moment.

1️⃣ Present Continuous (+)

Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing
I + am → I am playing.
He / She / It + is → She is playing.
You / We / They + are → They are playing.

2️⃣ More Examples

📚 Emma is reading a book.
⚽ Ben and Leo are playing football.
📝 I am doing my homework.

3️⃣ Verb + ING 🔎

Most verbs → + ing

play → playing
read → reading
Final -e → remove e

make → making
write → writing
Some short verbs → double

run → running
swim → swimming
⭐ Watch out!
❌ She is play football.
✅ She is playing football.

1️⃣ Present Continuous (-)

Subject + am / is / are + not + verb-ing
I am not sleeping.
She isn't sleeping.
They aren't sleeping.
is not = isn't
are not = aren't

2️⃣ Make it Negative 🔄

Emma is reading a book.
Emma isn't reading a book.
Ben and Leo are playing football.
Ben and Leo aren't playing football.
I am watching TV.
I am not watching TV.

✏️ Quick Practice — Negative

1. Mia watching TV. (-)
2. They eating dinner. (-)
3. I sleeping. (-)
4. Ben running. (-)

1️⃣ Present Continuous (?)

Am / Is / Are + subject + verb-ing?
Are you reading?
Is Emma sleeping?
Are they playing?

2️⃣ Short Answers

Is Ben playing football?
✅ Yes, he is.
❌ No, he isn't.

Are they eating?
✅ Yes, they are.
❌ No, they aren't.

3️⃣ What are they doing? 💬

What are you doing?
I'm doing my homework.
What is Mia doing?
She's taking a picture.
