Past Continuous 🚀

An action in progress at a time in the past • was / were + verb-ing

🌟 What would you like to do?

Learn the rule. Practise it. Build your own sentences!

📘 Learn: Past Continuous

Think about a time in the past. An action was happening at that time.

At eight o’clock last night, Emma was reading a book.

📖 Reading at a time in the past

⏰ Time Expressions

at eight o’clock last nightat this time yesterdayyesterday at five o’clock

Say the time and tell us what was happening then. “Yesterday” alone does not tell us that an action was in progress.

1. Make a positive sentence

Subject + was / were + verb-ing
I / He / She / It → wasI was reading at eight o’clock last night.
You / We / They → wereThey were playing yesterday at five o’clock.
She was eating lunch yesterday at one o’clock.
We were doing homework at six o’clock last night.
It was sleeping at nine o’clock last night.

2. Add -ing

Most verbs: add -ingplay → playing
read → reading
Take away the final emake → making
write → writing
Double the last letter in these wordsrun → running
swim → swimming
⭐ Watch out!
✗ She was play.
✓ She was playing.
✗ They was playing.
✓ They were playing.
Think about an action at five o’clock yesterday.

3. Look, think and tap

Read the picture clue and time. Tap to see the full sentence.

1. Make a negative sentence

Subject + was / were + not + verb-ing
was not = wasn’tI / He / She / It
were not = weren’tYou / We / They
Keep the -ing!
She wasn’t playing at five o’clock yesterday.
✗ She wasn’t play.

2. Make it negative

1. Move was / were to the front

Was / Were + subject + verb-ing?
At eight o’clock last night
Shewasreading.
↓ Move “was” before “she”
Wasshereading?
Were they playing yesterday at five o’clock?
Was Ben sleeping at nine o’clock last night?

2. Short answers

Choose Yes or No to match what was happening.

Was he reading at eight o’clock last night?Yes, he was.
No, he wasn’t.
Were they playing yesterday at five o’clock?Yes, they were.
No, they weren’t.

3. Ask What or Where

What was she doing at eight o’clock last night?
She was reading a book.
What were they doing yesterday at five o’clock?
They were playing football.
Where were they playing yesterday at five o’clock?
They were playing in the park.
